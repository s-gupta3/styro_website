FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the effective range of Styro electric?

+

When completely charged, the vehicle is capable of traveling distances of 25 kilometers and 50 kilometers.

What is the total charging time required for the vehicle?

+

The fast charging process for the model with a range of 25 kilometres is completed in a duration of 3 hours, and the model with a 50-kilometre range model is fully charged in 4 hours.

What is the warranty period?

+

There is a one-year warranty on the motor, battery, and charger.

What is the weight of the vehicle?

+

The 25-kilometer range model weighs 16 kg, and the 50-kilometer range model weighs 18 kg.

When will pre-orders ship?

+

We are targeting to begin shipping for all pre-orders in the January of 2026. You will receive regular updates on our progress after you reserve your vehicle.

Is it durable and safe?

+

Stryo Electric has undergone rigorous engineering and testing at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee over a span of three years, ensuring its durability and safety.

Is it water-resistant?

+

Yes, the Stryo Electric has an IP54 rating, which means it is resistant to water splashes from any direction. While it can handle light rain, we do not recommend riding through deep puddles or submerging it in water.

How can we arrange for our vehicle to be serviced and repaired?

+

Spare parts will be available on our website, and DIY videos will remain accessible until service centers open in your city.
