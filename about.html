OUR MISSION

At Stryo Electric, our mission is to redefine the urban commute. We believe that personal transport should be seamless, sustainable, and, above all, inspiring. We are committed to creating innovative electric vehicles that not only solve the challenges of city travel but also bring a sense of freedom and excitement to the daily journey. We’re not just building a product; we’re building a new way to experience the city.

OUR STORY

Born from a passion for design and a frustration with conventional transport, Stryo Electric started as a bold idea: what if your vehicle could adapt to your life, not the other way around? This question led us down a path of relentless innovation, culminating in the Stryo Fold. Our journey is driven by a team of visionary engineers and designers dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what's possible in personal electric mobility. We are thinkers, creators, and riders, united by the goal of making our cities more accessible and our lives more extraordinary.
